Questions

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your order is saved on this phone or laptop. Keep this page, or note your pickup code.

How do I collect my prints?

Wait until your order says Ready, then show the pickup code at the counter. Your code is also printed small on the corner of your first page, so staff find your pages in seconds.

Can I print only some pages of a big PDF?

Yes. Choose Choose pages and type the pages, for example 102 or 333-390 or 1-5, 8. You only pay for those pages.

Are my files private?

Files are stored privately, can only be downloaded by the Xerox center printer, and are deleted after printing.

Something went wrong after I paid?

Your payment is safe. Show your pickup code at the counter: staff will print it again or refund you.

Can I add this to my home screen?

Yes. On Android tap Install app at the top (or the browser menu → Add to Home screen). On iPhone tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
