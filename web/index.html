Questions

Can I print several files in one order?

Yes. Add as many PDFs, Word files and photos as you like, at once or one after another, and set each one up on its own: which pages, how many copies, colour or black & white, one- or two-sided, paper size, pages per sheet and more. You pay once and collect everything together.You press Print once and collect everything together.

How many pages can I print?

Your free pages for the month are shown at the top. One printed side is one page, so a two-sided sheet counts as two. The count starts again on the 1st of every month. Pages that did not print, or that the Xerox center cancelled, are not counted.

Can I print only some pages of a big PDF?

Yes. Tap the pages you want on the thumbnails, or type them, for example 3, 7, 10-12. You only pay for those pages.Only those pages are counted.

Why are some options missing or marked?

You only see what the printers at the Xerox center can really do right now. If an option only works with another choice (for example two-sided only in black & white), it says so, and picking it changes the other choice for you.

Will my photo be stretched?

Never. Photos keep their proportions: Fit shows the whole photo, Fill covers the page and trims the edges. The preview shows exactly what the paper will look like.

How do I collect my prints?

After you payAfter you press Print, your order shows a picture of every file and the time it will be ready. At the counter, open the order on this phoneyour phone and tap I’m at the counter: your files appear on the Xerox center’s screen and they hand you the pages that look the same. There is no code to remember or type.

Can a friend collect for me?

The order opens only on the phone that made it. Give that phone to your friend, or collect later: your prints wait for you at the counter.

I sent it from the computer. Can I collect with my phone?

Yes. Sign in with the same staff ID on your phone (this page, or the staff app): everything you sent is under Your prints. Keep your staff ID to yourself: every page printed with it counts as yours.

What if my phone has no internet at the counter?

Your order still opens and shows your files. Tell the staff the file’s name: they find the order on their screen and hand you the pages.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your order is saved on this phone or laptop: open this page again on the same device to see it.

How do I get a staff ID, or a new password?

At the Xerox center. They make your staff ID: a username and a password, nothing else. Forgot the password? They make a new one in a minute; the old one then stops working everywhere.

Are my files private?

Files are stored privately, can only be downloaded by the Xerox center printer, and are deleted after printing. Until you collect, a small picture of each file’s first page is kept so staff can match your pages.

Something went wrong after I paid?

Your payment is safe. Show your order at the counter: staff will print it again or refund you.

Something went wrong with a print?

Show your order at the counter: they will print it again. Pages that did not print are not counted.
