Questions

Can I print several files in one order?

Yes. Add as many PDFs and photos as you like, at once or one after another, and set each one up on its own: which pages, how many copies, colour or black & white, one- or two-sided, paper size, pages per sheet and more. You pay once and collect everything with one pickup code.

Can I print only some pages of a big PDF?

Yes. Tap the pages you want on the thumbnails, or type them, for example 3, 7, 10-12. You only pay for those pages.

Why are some options missing or marked?

You only see what the printers at the Xerox center can really do right now. If an option only works with another choice (for example two-sided only in black & white), it says so, and picking it changes the other choice for you.

Will my photo be stretched?

Never. Photos keep their proportions: Fit shows the whole photo, Fill covers the page and trims the edges. The preview shows exactly what the paper will look like.

How do I collect my prints?

Wait until your order says Ready, then show the pickup code at the counter. The code is also printed small on the corner of the first page of each file, so staff find your pages in seconds.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your order is saved on this phone or laptop. Keep this page, or note your pickup code.

Are my files private?

Files are stored privately, can only be downloaded by the Xerox center printer, and are deleted after printing.

Something went wrong after I paid?

Your payment is safe. Show your pickup code at the counter: staff will print it again or refund you.
